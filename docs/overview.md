# TEGO — Project Overview

## What is TEGO?

TEGO is a Web3 community project built around a utility token and a growing suite of products. It's designed to create a clear direction, real user interaction and lasting community value — without focusing on short-term noise or unnecessary complexity.

TEGO operates with a small, focused team committed to building real products and maintaining honest communication at every stage of development.

---

## Core Products

### TEGO Token

TEGO is designed as a utility-driven token intended to support engagement, participation and progression.

- Supports user activity and progression within TEGO Quest
- Designed to reward consistent participation over passive holding
- Token-based incentives will be progressively connected to platform activity
- Allocation structured with community-first priorities and team lock mechanisms

### TEGO Quest

TEGO Quest is a gamified Telegram Mini App — the first live product in the TEGO project.

Users complete daily tasks, earn points, build streaks and compete on leaderboards. The platform turns passive followers into active participants through a simple, consistent engagement loop.

**Core mechanics:**
- Daily tasks and challenges
- Points and XP accumulation
- Streak tracking
- Community leaderboards
- Progression system with levels and rewards

## Mission

Build a space people recognize, engage with and choose to stay in.

TEGO focuses on:

1. **Honest communication** — No overpromising. No hype cycles. The team shares real progress and reports setbacks openly.
2. **Real products** — TEGO Quest is a live, functional product. Development is ongoing and iterative.
3. **Community first** — Decisions are guided by what genuinely serves holders and participants.
4. **Steady progress** — Consistent, reliable development over reactive short-term moves.
